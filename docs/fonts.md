## Typography Options

Choose the typography direction that best fits the brand after reviewing the existing logo, colors, imagery, and overall redesign.

Do NOT use Tailwind typography or introduce unnecessary font libraries.

Preferred options:

### Option 1 — Heebo + Assistant
- Headings: Heebo 700–800
- Body: Assistant 400–600
- Personality: Clean, trustworthy, Israeli, modern
- Best for: Safe all-around choice

### Option 2 — Rubik + Heebo
- Headings: Rubik 700–800
- Body: Heebo 400–600
- Personality: Modern, energetic, friendly, movement-oriented
- Best for: Youthful and social movement feel

### Option 3 — Noto Sans Hebrew
- Headings: Noto Sans Hebrew 700–800
- Body: Noto Sans Hebrew 400–500
- Personality: Neutral, clean, highly readable
- Best for: Content-focused design where photography/branding carries the personality

### Option 4 — Alef + Heebo
- Headings: Alef 700
- Body: Heebo 400–600
- Personality: Warm, approachable, distinctly Israeli
- Best for: Human and community-oriented feel

### Option 5 — Secular One + Heebo
- Hero/display headlines: Secular One
- Section headings: Secular One or Heebo 700
- Body: Heebo 400–600
- Personality: Bold, memorable, campaign-like
- Best for: Making "לשון הרע לא מדבר אליי" feel like a strong movement slogan

### Option 6 — Frank Ruhl Libre + Heebo
- Editorial/headline sections: Frank Ruhl Libre 700
- Body: Heebo 400–600
- Personality: Editorial, emotional, established
- Best for: Stories, history, and editorial content
- Use selectively rather than across the entire website

## Recommendation

Evaluate all six options and choose the strongest one for this specific brand.

The final typography system should generally have:

- Display/Hero: 700–800
- H1: 700–800
- H2/H3: 600–700
- Body: 400–500
- Buttons/Navigation: 600–700
- Captions/secondary text: 400–500

Prioritize:
1. Hebrew readability
2. Brand personality
3. Accessibility
4. Clear visual hierarchy
5. Performance
6. Consistency across desktop and mobile

Avoid using more than 2 font families unless there is a strong design reason.

Before implementing, briefly explain which option you selected and why.
